Epicure ingredient embeddings

Ingredient swaps, “more like this” and the cuisine lean use Epicure-Core by Jakub Radzikowski and Josef Chen (KAIKAKU.AI), © 2026, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

Paper: Radzikowski, J. and Chen, J. (2026). Epicure: Navigating the Emergent Geometry of Food Ingredient Embeddings. arXiv:2605.22391.

Version used: revision d31ebb5af8e92bbaf5cb67381d5006d4ea8368b7 (files: embeddings.safetensors, vocab.json, itos.json, modes.json, factor_poles.npy, cuisine_pole_provenance.json).

Changes made: Ziggy does not modify the files. It normalises the vectors for cosine similarity, averages them into dish and cuisine directions, and ranks a hand-labelled list of everyday Indian pantry ingredients with them. Allergen and diet safety comes from Ziggy’s own labels, not from the embeddings. The test suite ships a small subset of the vectors (some ingredients only) under the same licence.

The authors do not endorse Ziggy.
