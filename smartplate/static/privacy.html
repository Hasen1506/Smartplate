Privacy notice

Version {{NOTICE_VERSION}}. Written for India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025. In this notice “Ziggy”, “we” and “us” mean {{OPERATOR}}, the Data Fiduciary for your data.

The short version

  • Ziggy plans your meals from what you tell it: diet, allergies, budget, meal times and favourite places.
  • We use your data only to plan and order your meals. We don’t sell it, show you ads, or send it to any AI service.
  • Health details and Swiggy access are optional. Each is asked for separately, and you can switch it off any time.
  • Download everything or delete your profile in More → Account & devices → Your data. Deleting is immediate.
  • Ziggy is for adults (18+).
  • Questions or complaints: {{CONTACT}}. We reply within 7 days and always within 90.

What we collect, and why

Each group below is a separate purpose. You agree to the first when you create a profile; the others are separate yes/no choices.

1. Your profile and meal plans (needed to use Ziggy)

  • Data: the profile name you choose; diet type (veg, egg, non-veg, vegan); allergies and foods you avoid; weekly budget and meal times; how often you cook; favourite restaurants and dishes; the plans Ziggy makes, the meals you pick, rate or skip, and spending you record; an optional sign-in name and password (we store only a one-way hash of the password); people in your household you plan for (name, diet and allergies only).
  • Why: to build weekly plans that respect your diet, allergies, budget and timing, and to keep them between visits.
  • Allergies are used as a hard safety filter. A meal whose ingredients can’t be checked is hidden, not guessed.

2. Health details for nutrition (optional)

  • Data: medical conditions you pick (for example diabetes or high blood pressure), and height, weight, age, sex and activity level if you add them.
  • Why: to keep meals within your health limits and show nutrition targets.
  • If you say no or switch it off: these details are deleted and plans ignore them. Allergies still apply.

3. Learning from your ratings (optional)

  • Data: the reasons you tap after a meal (arrived late, small portion, too spicy, too pricey, great).
  • Why: to make later plans fit you better. Ziggy shows each thing it learned, with Undo.
  • If you switch it off: the saved reasons are deleted and new ones are not saved.

4. Connecting Swiggy (optional, asked right before you connect)

  • Data: a Swiggy access token (encrypted), your saved Swiggy delivery addresses (label, area and the address Swiggy returns), live menus and prices for the address you choose, your Swiggy cart, and your recent Swiggy orders (restaurants, dishes, prices, dates).
  • Why: to show real menus and prices, add a meal to your Swiggy cart, place an order only after you approve the exact total, and suggest your usual dishes from past orders.
  • If you switch it off: we disconnect, delete the token, the menus and cart details we kept, and plan without live menus. Your Swiggy account and its orders are Swiggy’s and stay with Swiggy.

Always, for security

  • Data: a session cookie, sign-in device names (“Phone”, “Computer”) and when they were last used, rate-limit counters keyed to your IP address, and short server logs (request path, time, status, a request id).
  • Why: to keep your profile private to you and to stop password guessing and abuse.

We never ask for card or UPI details. Ziggy does not take payments.

Who else handles your data

These companies process data for us under their terms, only to run Ziggy:

  • Render (render.com) runs the Ziggy server, in its Singapore region.
  • Neon (neon.tech) hosts the database on Amazon Web Services in Singapore (ap-southeast-1).
  • Push services run by your browser’s maker (Google, Mozilla, Microsoft or Apple) deliver reminders if you turn them on. They receive an encrypted message and a delivery address for your browser.

Swiggy (Bundl Technologies) is a separate company with its own privacy policy. When you connect, Ziggy reads from and writes to your Swiggy account on your instruction. What Swiggy does with your Swiggy data is governed by Swiggy’s policy, not this notice.

The weather forecast is fetched from Open-Meteo for your city only; no personal data is sent. Fonts are served by Ziggy itself. There are no analytics or advertising trackers.

Your data is stored and processed in Singapore. The DPDP Act allows this unless the Government restricts a country; Singapore is not restricted today. If that changes, we will move the data or tell you.

How long we keep it

  • Your profile and plans: until you delete them. {{INACTIVE_RULE}}
  • Swiggy token and menus: until you disconnect, switch Swiggy off, or Swiggy ends the sign-in.
  • Sign-in sessions: end after 60 days without use.
  • Record of your consent choices: kept while your profile exists and for one year after you delete it, as the DPDP Rules require for processing logs. It holds your profile number, the choices and their dates, never your name, diet or health details.
  • Database history: our database host keeps a rolling history for recovery ({{BACKUP_WINDOW}}). Deleted data leaves that history when the window passes.

Your rights, and how to use them

  • See your data: More → Account & devices → Your data → Download my data gives you everything we hold about your profile as a file. You can also ask us for a summary and the list of companies above.
  • Correct it: change any answer in More → Food & budget.
  • Withdraw consent: switch health details, learning or Swiggy off in More → Privacy, as easily as you switched them on. Processing stops and the related data is deleted.
  • Erase it: More → Account & devices → Your data → Delete my Ziggy profile deletes your profile and everything linked to it straight away.
  • Nominate someone to use these rights if you die or can’t act: write to us at the contact below.
  • Complain: write to our grievance contact below with your profile number (the part of your recovery code before the dot, shown in Account & devices). We reply within 7 days and always within 90 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India through its online portal.

How we protect it

  • Connections use HTTPS. Your session cookie can’t be read by page scripts and is replaced every day you use Ziggy.
  • Swiggy tokens are encrypted with a key kept outside the database. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes.
  • Profiles are private: only browsers you signed in on, or that hold your recovery code, can open them.
  • Error messages and logs about Swiggy replies have addresses, phone numbers and tokens removed.
  • If a breach affects your data we will tell you in the app, and the Data Protection Board, without delay.
