What Ziggy is

Ziggy is a meal planner. It suggests what to eat across the week from your favourites, your rules (diet, allergies, budget, ratings, delivery time) and, when you connect it, live Swiggy menus. It is an early pilot: features change, and the service may be slow or unavailable at times.

Who can use it

You must be 18 or older. Use Ziggy for your own meals and the people you cook or order for. Don’t try to open profiles that aren’t yours, overload the service, or use it to break Swiggy’s terms.

Orders and prices

  • Ziggy never places an order without your approval of the exact items, address, total and payment method.
  • Orders are placed with Swiggy and are between you, Swiggy and the restaurant. Swiggy’s terms, cancellation and refund rules apply. Ziggy is not a party to the sale and does not take payment.
  • Prices marked “est.” are estimates. Only the total read back from your Swiggy cart is real. Budgets in Ziggy are targets; they can’t stop you spending more in the Swiggy app.

Food safety and health

  • Ziggy hides dishes whose ingredients break your allergies or diet when it can tell. Menus often don’t list every ingredient, so always check with the restaurant if an allergy is serious.
  • Nutrition figures are estimates, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or dietitian about medical diets.
